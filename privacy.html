6. Subscriptions and payments

Paid subscriptions are purchased through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google processes the payment; we never see your card details. RevenueCat is used to manage subscription status; your in-app user ID and purchase information are shared with RevenueCat.

[[DOĞRULA: RevenueCat entegre edildi — paket kurulu ama kodda hiç çağrılmıyor; abonelik henüz satışta değil]] [[DOĞRULA: RevenueCat'e giden veri, konumu ve saklama süresi]]

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

7. Retention

  • Account, profile, wardrobe, outfits, favorites, quota and usage counters: for as long as your account exists. Deleted immediately when you delete your account (see Delete Your Account).
  • Individually deleted clothing items: the record is removed from your wardrobe immediately. [[DOĞRULA: kıyafet silinince görsel dosyası storage'dan silinmiyor — hesap silinene kadar kalıyor; kod düzeltilecek mi]]

8. Your rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed; to request information if it is; to learn the purpose of processing and whether data is used accordingly; to know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom it is transferred; to request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data; to request deletion or destruction under KVKK Art. 7; to request that corrections and deletions be notified to those third parties; to object to an outcome against you arising solely from automated analysis; and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

If the GDPR applies to you, you also have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection to processing based on legitimate interest, and to withdraw consent at any time.

In the App you can edit your name, physical profile and wardrobe, and delete your account. For other requests, write from your registered email address to support@sestinaapp.com. We respond within 30 days at the latest.

You may lodge a complaint with Turkey’s Personal Data Protection Board or, if you are in the EU, with your local data protection authority. [[DOĞRULA: KVKK başvuru usulü (Veri Sorumlusuna Başvuru Usul ve Esasları Tebliği) — e-posta başvurusu için kimlik doğrulama yöntemi]]

9. Children

Sestina is not intended for anyone under 18, and people under 18 should not create an account. If we learn that we are processing data of someone under 18, we will delete the account and its data; you can report this to support@sestinaapp.com.

[[DOĞRULA: kayıtta yaş beyanı/kontrolü yok; fiziksel profildeki yaş seçici 16'dan başlıyor — 18'e çekilecek mi]]

10. Security

Connections between the App, our servers and service providers are encrypted (HTTPS). The database enforces row-level access rules, so each user can only access their own records. Third-party API keys are kept on the server only. Quota and usage counters cannot be changed from the App. No system is completely secure; if a data breach occurs, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page and the date at the top will change. We will announce significant changes in the App or by email.

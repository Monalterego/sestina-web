4. Explicit consent for virtual try-on

Virtual try-on relies on two separate explicit consents: your photo (your profile photo being sent to FASHN) and your physical details (being sent to FASHN to generate an AI mannequin). Before you upload a profile photo or save your physical profile for the first time, the App shows the notice; consent cannot be given before you tap “I've read this”. The consents are independent; you can agree to just one. If you don’t consent, the related data is not saved and you can keep using the rest of the App.

You can withdraw each consent separately in Profile → Privacy & permissions:

  • Photo consent: your photo and the try-on images created with it are deleted; your physical details and AI mannequin stay.
  • Physical details consent: your physical details, the AI mannequin generated from them and the try-on images created with the mannequin are deleted; your photo stays.

Users who saved this data before the consents were split are asked once again for the consents needed; data tied to a consent that isn’t given is deleted. Full consent texts and versions: Consent Texts.

Which data is transferred for which purpose: Privacy Notice, Transfers abroad.

8. Subscriptions and payments

Paid subscriptions are purchased through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google processes the payment; we never see your card details. We receive and store your subscription status through RevenueCat (product, store, period type, expiry date, auto-renewal). Plan limits are set out in the Terms of Use. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

10. Your rights and downloading your data

Your rights under KVKK Art. 11 are listed in the Privacy Notice. In the App you can do the following yourself:

  • Profile → Download my data: your profile, style profile, wardrobe details, outfits, favorites, consent history and subscription status are prepared as a machine-readable file (JSON); download links for your photos, valid for 1 hour, are included. Up to 3 times a day.
  • Edit your name, physical profile and wardrobe details; delete clothing items and your profile photo; withdraw permissions in Profile → Privacy & permissions; delete your account.

For other requests: Requests. We respond within 30 days at the latest, free of charge.

12. Children

Sestina is for users aged 18 and over. Whichever way you sign in (email, Apple, Google), you are asked to confirm that you are over 18 before using the App. If you indicate that you are under 18, deletion of your account and all your data is started. If we otherwise learn that we are processing data of someone under 18, we will delete the account and its data; you can report this to support@sestinaapp.com.

13. Security

Connections between the App, our servers and service providers are encrypted (HTTPS). The database and file storage enforce row-level access rules, so each user can only access their own records and files. Photos are kept in private storage and opened only through time-limited signed links. Third-party API keys are kept on the server only. Quota counters and consent records cannot be changed from the App. No system is completely secure; if a data breach occurs, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

14. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page and the date at the top will change. We will announce significant changes in the App or by email. Each consent record stores the version of the text you accepted; if the virtual try-on notice changes, you are asked to read the current text again before giving a new consent.

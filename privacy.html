3. Explicit consent for physical profile and photo

Before you save your physical profile or upload a profile photo for the first time, the App shows an explicit consent screen. It explains what is collected, what it is used for, and that it is sent to FASHN. If you don’t consent, this information is not saved; you can keep using the rest of the App.

You can withdraw your consent at any time via Profile → Withdraw My Consent. When you do, your physical information, profile photo, AI mannequin and all virtual try-on images are deleted; your wardrobe and outfits remain.

7. Subscriptions and payments

Paid subscriptions are purchased through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google processes the payment; we never see your card details. RevenueCat is used to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives your user ID (our account ID, not your email or name) and the purchase information reported by the store. Our server fetches your subscription status from RevenueCat and stores it (product, store, period type, expiry date, auto-renewal). When you delete your account, your subscriber record at RevenueCat is deleted too. Plan limits are set out in the Terms of Use.

[[DOĞRULA: RevenueCat konumu ve saklama süresi]]

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

8. Retention

  • Account, style profile, wardrobe, outfits, favorites, quota and usage counters: for as long as your account exists. Deleted immediately when you delete your account (see Delete Your Account).
  • Individually deleted clothing items: the record, its images and any virtual try-on images containing that item are deleted immediately.
  • Profile photo: until you remove it. Removing it also deletes your virtual try-on result images; the AI mannequin generated from your physical profile remains.
  • Physical profile and AI mannequin: until you withdraw consent or delete your account.
  • Store scan photos, original clothing photos, location: not stored on our servers.

9. Your rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed; to request information if it is; to learn the purpose of processing and whether data is used accordingly; to know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom it is transferred; to request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data; to request deletion or destruction under KVKK Art. 7; to request that corrections and deletions be notified to those third parties; to object to an outcome against you arising solely from automated analysis; and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

If the GDPR applies to you, you also have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection to processing based on legitimate interest, and to withdraw consent at any time.

In the App you can edit your name, physical profile and wardrobe; delete clothing items and your profile photo; withdraw your explicit consent; and delete your account. For other requests, write from your registered email address to support@sestinaapp.com. We respond within 30 days at the latest.

You may lodge a complaint with Turkey’s Personal Data Protection Board or, if you are in the EU, with your local data protection authority. [[DOĞRULA: KVKK başvuru usulü (Veri Sorumlusuna Başvuru Usul ve Esasları Tebliği) — e-posta başvurusu için kimlik doğrulama yöntemi]]

10. Children

Sestina is for users aged 18 and over. Whichever way you sign in (email, Apple, Google), you are asked to confirm that you are over 18 before using the App. If you indicate that you are under 18, your account and all your data are deleted. If we otherwise learn that we are processing data of someone under 18, we will delete the account and its data; you can report this to support@sestinaapp.com.

11. Security

Connections between the App, our servers and service providers are encrypted (HTTPS). The database and file storage enforce row-level access rules, so each user can only access their own records and files. Photos are kept in private storage and opened only through time-limited signed links. Third-party API keys are kept on the server only. Quota and usage counters cannot be changed from the App. No system is completely secure; if a data breach occurs, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

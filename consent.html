1. AI permission (ai_processing · ai-processing-2026-10-20)

AI PERMISSION

Can we process your photos with AI?

When you add clothes and use “Match?” scans, we send your photo to two service providers. We ask for your permission before sending anything.

What data?
Only the photo of the clothing item you add or the product you scan. If you allow this, the names of the styles you pick are also sent to Anthropic to create your Style DNA name. Your own photo, physical details, name and email address are not sent.

To whom, and why?
Poof · Germany — To remove the photo's background.
Anthropic · USA — To recognise the item's features such as category, colour, pattern and season.

Model training
The photos we send are not used to train these providers' AI models.

If you don't allow it
You can keep using the app. Clothing photos are saved as they are (background not removed), and you choose the category, colour and season yourself. “Match?” scans stay off. You can change your choice at any time in Profile → Privacy & permissions.

Privacy Policy

[Allow] [Not now]

Description on the Profile → Privacy & permissions screen:

AI photo processing
Your clothing and product photos are sent to Poof (Germany) for background removal and to Anthropic (USA) for feature recognition.

If you withdraw it, your existing clothes and their details are not deleted. New clothes are saved with the photo as it is, and you choose the category, colour and season. “Match?” scans turn off.

2. Virtual try-on: notice and two separate explicit consents

The virtual try-on screen first shows the notice (“Privacy notice”). The consent boxes cannot be ticked until you tap “I've read this”. Consent for your photo and for your physical details are independent; only the consent needed is asked.

2.1 Notice (notice_shown · tryon-notice-2026-10-20)

Virtual try-on

We only process personal details for virtual try-on with your explicit consent. Please read the notice below first.

PRIVACY NOTICE

What data?
Your full-length photo, if you upload one; your physical details, if you fill them in (height, weight, age, body type, skin tone, hair colour, length and type).

What for?
Only for virtual try-on: to dress your clothes on your photo or on an AI mannequin generated from your physical details. They are not used for outfit suggestions.

Who receives it?
The try-on image is created by the image-generation service FASHN. Your photo is sent to FASHN via a link valid for 10 minutes; your physical details are sent as a description for generating an AI mannequin. Your own photo and physical details are not sent to Anthropic or Poof.

Storage and control
This data is kept in your account for as long as your consent lasts. You can withdraw each consent separately in Profile → Privacy & permissions; the data tied to that consent and the images created with it are deleted.

Your rights
You can access your data and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Use Profile → Download my data to get a copy, or Delete My Account to delete all of it.

3. Permission status and withdrawal

The Profile → Privacy & permissions screen shows your latest choice and its date for each permission (“Allowed · date”, “Not allowed · date” or “No choice made yet”). You can change each one separately with “Allow” or “Withdraw permission”. Details: Privacy Policy, Privacy Notice.

This notice is provided under Article 10 of Turkey’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”) and the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles for Fulfilling the Obligation to Inform, to inform users of the Sestina mobile app (the “App”). More detail is in the Privacy Policy.

The data controller is Hasan Can Demircan, the individual developer of the App. Contact: support@sestinaapp.com.

3. Recipients

Your personal data is shared with the following service providers only as far as needed for the purposes above: Supabase (hosting), Anthropic (AI clothing recognition and Style DNA name), Poof (background removal), FASHN (virtual try-on), OpenWeather (weather), RevenueCat (subscription management), Expo (app updates), Apple and Google (sign-in and store purchases). The data sent to each is listed in the Privacy Policy. Where legally required, it may also be shared with public authorities entitled to request it.

6. Your rights under KVKK Art. 11

By applying to the data controller, you can:

  • Learn whether your personal data is processed,
  • Request information if it is,
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly,
  • Know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom it is transferred,
  • Request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate,
  • Request deletion or destruction under the conditions in KVKK Art. 7,
  • Request that correction, deletion and destruction be notified to the third parties to whom it was transferred,
  • Object to an outcome against you arising solely from analysis by automated systems,
  • Claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing.

7. How to apply

How to apply, the information requested and the deadlines are explained on the Requests page. You can get a copy of your data right away in the App via Profile → Download my data.
